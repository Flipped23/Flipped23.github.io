About

I am an undergraduate student in the Department of Automation at Tsinghua University. I will be a direct-entry Ph.D. student in the Department of Automation at Tsinghua University in 2027, advised by Prof. Song-Chun Zhu. My research interests lie broadly in LLM agents, multi-agent systems, cognitive AI, explainable AI, multimodal models, post-training, and evaluation. I am particularly interested in how intelligent systems can understand people and their latent states, reason and adapt through interaction, and make reliable decisions over extended contexts. My current work explores topics including human value and preference understanding, longitudinal person modeling, agent evaluation, multimodal reasoning, and post-training for large language models.

I am always open to discussing new ideas and exploring potential collaborations. If you are interested in related research directions, feel free to reach out.
